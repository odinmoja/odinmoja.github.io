Research

My current interests and focus lay in the realms of machine learning, computer vision, generative AI, optimisation problems, and synthetic data. I am focused on the use of models in realms of daily impact such as medicine, finance, engineering, agriculture and scientific imaging to name a few. I have broad range of personal interests which often leads to me collaborating as part of a multidisciplinary team. Below is a collection of my conference and journal papers.

The Machine's Eye Does Not Tire: Experimental Exploration of Synthetic Data in Subject Detection of Florida Manatees

Moja, O. O. (2026). The Machine’s Eye Does Not Tire: Experimental Exploration of Synthetic Data in Subject Detection of Florida Manatees [PhD Thesis, The Florida State University].

My PhD dissertation in which I leveraged Flux.2 generative AI with a LoRA to supplement and augment genuine training data for the YOLO object detection model. I achieved promising results and synthetic data proved to be useful in aiding the already lightweight, robust performance of YOLO.

Comparison of Authentic Media and Synthetic Media in Computer Vision Training

Adams, J., Moja, O., & Ko, C. (2026, March 13–14). Comparison of authentic media and synthetic media in computer vision training [Paper presentation]. 28th Annual Southern Association for Information Systems Conference, Asheville, NC, United States. aisnet.org

Air-sea rescue is a highly time sensitive endeavour. Victim submersion time strongly influences survival time. The problem is exasperated by the vast search spaces presented by the ocean. Our study used the combination of several tools to produce an automated synthetic data pipeline which both generated and labelled images. Our results showed the model performed well even in comparison to studies using purely authentic data.

Complex questions and quality answers: Comparing ChatGPT and Gemini as research collaborators

Ravuri, B., Mardis, M. A.,Moja, O., Ayinde, L., & Emdad, F. B. (2026). Complex questions and quality answers: Comparing CHATGPT and Gemini as research collaborators. Journal of the Association for Information Science and Technology, asi.70056. https://doi.org/10.1002/asi.70056

Exploring and comparing the performance of major Large Language Models (LLMs) as research collaborators. Our analyses demonstrated a strong, statistically significant association between question complexity (READ) levels and further assistance.

Uses of Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education.

Adams, J., Roberts, A., Obermaier, L., Ravuri, B., & Moja, O. (2023). Uses of Artificial Intelligence in Higher Education. EdMedia, 1181–1186. https://www.learntechlib.org/p/222633/

This paper investigates the distribution and focal areas of research being conducted in higher education between the years of 2019 and January, 2023. The analysis examined 723 articles resulting from a structured search of the Gale Onefile, ERIC, and Education Full Text databases. The search strategy included peer-reviewed publications published after 2019, filtered for key terms that included artificial intelligence, higher education, and teaching.

Issues and a proposal for automated retirement portfolio risk matching in South Africa

Moja, O. O., & Malan, K. M. (2018). Issues and a proposal for automated retirement portfolio risk matching in South Africa. 2018 Conference on Information Communications Technology and Society (ICTAS), 1–6.

This paper is an investigation into the issues related to introducing automation to portfolio optimisation for risk matching.
